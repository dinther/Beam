Based On:


ASLS Studio

Beatline Beam is a fork of ASLS Studio by Timé Kadel, released under the GNU General Public License v3.0. The patching, scene, effect and chase engines, the UI kit and the original visualizer are its work. Beam adds Art-Net input, the rebuilt visualizer, generic LED fixtures, group mappings and the MadMapper layout and library export.

https://github.com/ASLS-org/studio

Gobo images

44 of the gobo images in gobos/ are from the Open Fixture Library's resources/gobos collection, MIT licensed. Most were collected by OFL from QLC+ (Apache License 2.0), some from a Thomann product manual; OFL's repository names each image's source. Gobo designs drawn from manufacturers' catalogues (Chauvet, Clay Paky, SGM and others) remain those manufacturers' designs.

https://github.com/OpenLightingProject/open-fixture-library/tree/master/resources/gobos
https://github.com/mcallegari/qlcplus

The other gobo images in gobos/ and the animation discs in animation/ other than Flames, Moving and Warbly are taken from the wheel images in manufacturers' GDTF files (ADJ, Ayrton, Clay Paky, Elation, Martin Professional, Robe Lighting, TIITEE, Tomshine, Vari-Lite and Wisdom). The designs remain those manufacturers'.

https://gdtf-share.com
